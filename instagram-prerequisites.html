✓ Professional Account (Business or Creator)

Your Instagram account must be a Professional account to use messaging automation.

Show instructions ↓

How to check if you have a Professional account:

  1. Open Instagram app on your phone
  2. Go to your profile
  3. Tap the menu (☰) in the top right
  4. Look for "Professional dashboard" or "Insights"
  5. If you see these, you already have a Professional account ✓

How to convert to Professional account:

  1. Go to your Instagram profile
  2. Tap menu (☰) → Settings and privacy
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Switch to professional account
  5. Choose Business or Creator
  6. Follow the setup steps (pick category, add contact info)
  7. Done! You now have a Professional account ✓

✓ Message Access Enabled

Allow people to send you messages and comments that REPLAI can respond to.

Show instructions ↓

How to enable Message Access:

  1. Open Instagram app
  2. Go to your profile
  3. Tap menu (☰) → Settings and privacy
  4. Tap Messages and story replies
  5. Under "Who can send you messages", make sure it's not set to "No one"
  6. Recommended: Choose "Everyone" or "People you follow"

Enable comment replies:

  1. Go to Settings and privacy → Hidden Words
  2. Make sure "Hide comments" is OFF (so people can comment)
  3. Go back to Settings → Privacy → Comments
  4. Set "Allow comments from" to "Everyone" or your preference
