Privacy Policy for REPLAI

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

1. Introduction

REPLAI ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered messaging hub that helps businesses automate customer conversations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you send messages to businesses using REPLAI, we collect:

  • Message content and conversation history
  • User ID from the messaging platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram)
  • Timestamp of messages

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide AI-powered responses to your inquiries
  • Improve our AI response quality
  • Help businesses understand customer needs
  • Maintain conversation history for context

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on the business's own server. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your conversation data is only accessible to:

  • The business you're communicating with
  • Our AI processing service (Groq API) for generating responses

6. Data Retention

Conversation logs are retained for as long as necessary to provide the service. Businesses can delete conversation history at any time through the admin panel.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of automated messaging

8. Third-Party Services

REPLAI integrates with:

  • WhatsApp Business API - governed by Meta's privacy policy
  • Instagram Messaging - governed by Meta's privacy policy
  • Telegram Bot API - governed by Telegram's privacy policy
  • Groq API - for AI processing, governed by Groq's privacy policy

9. Children's Privacy

REPLAI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
