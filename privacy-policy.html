Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2024

1. Introduction

REPLAI ("we", "our", or "us") operates an AI-powered messaging automation platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Business name, email, and contact information
  • Instagram, Telegram, and WhatsApp account connections
  • Custom AI training instructions for your business

2.2 Instagram Data

When you connect your Instagram Business account, we access:

  • Profile information: Username, profile picture, follower count
  • Messages: Direct messages sent to your business account
  • Comments: Comments on your posts (if you enable comment management)

Purpose: To automatically respond to customer inquiries on your behalf.

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

  • Message content (to generate AI responses)
  • Response history (to maintain conversation context)
  • Usage analytics (message counts, platform activity)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service: Automatically respond to customer messages using AI
  • Improve AI Responses: Train AI based on your business context and preferences
  • Platform Integration: Connect and manage your Instagram, Telegram, and WhatsApp accounts
  • Analytics: Show you message statistics and platform health

4. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell or share your data with third parties, except:

  • AI Providers: We send message content to AI services (Groq, OpenRouter, Together AI) to generate responses
  • Platform APIs: We communicate with Instagram, Telegram, and WhatsApp APIs to send/receive messages
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage: Your data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL database)
  • Encryption: All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access Control: Only your business can access your messages and settings
  • Passwords: Stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing

6. Instagram-Specific Privacy

6.1 Permissions We Request

  • instagram_business_basic: Access your business profile information
  • instagram_business_manage_messages: Read and send direct messages
  • instagram_business_manage_comments: Read and reply to comments

6.2 What We Do With Instagram Data

  • Read incoming customer messages
  • Generate AI-powered responses based on your business context
  • Send responses on your behalf
  • Store message history for conversation context

6.3 What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ We don't post on your behalf without explicit instruction
  • ❌ We don't access your followers' personal data
  • ❌ We don't share your Instagram data with other businesses
  • ❌ We don't use your data for advertising

7. Data Retention

  • Messages: Stored for 90 days or until you delete your account
  • Connection Tokens: Stored until you disconnect the platform
  • Account Data: Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update incorrect information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Disconnect: Revoke Instagram, Telegram, or WhatsApp access at any time
  • Export: Download your message history

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies:

  • Session Cookies: To keep you logged in
  • OAuth State: Temporary tokens for secure platform connections

We DO NOT use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for businesses and business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States (Render hosting, Supabase database) and other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the platform. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us:

  • Email: privacy@replai.com
  • Website: https://replai-xc95.onrender.com

14. Meta/Facebook Platform Policy Compliance

REPLAI complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Instagram Platform Policy. We use Instagram data solely to provide messaging automation services to business accounts that have explicitly authorized our application.

15. Data Protection Officer

For GDPR-related inquiries (if applicable in your jurisdiction), contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@replai.com.
