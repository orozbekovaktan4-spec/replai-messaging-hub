## Tips for Success

1. **Ask questions early** - 5 min chat > 2 hours stuck
2. **Test frequently** - Every 30 minutes, check your work
3. **Commit often** - Save progress every hour
4. **Read error messages** - They tell you what's wrong
5. **Help each other** - If your teammate is stuck, help them!
6. **Take breaks** - Step away every 90 minutes
7. **Have fun!** - We're building something cool 🚀
