## 📋 Review Status Meanings

### ✅ **Approved**
- Green checkmark icon
- Status: "Approved" or "Live"
- **Action:** Your WhatsApp integration is ready! Users can start messaging.

### ⏳ **In Review**
- Yellow clock icon
- Status: "In Review" or "Pending"
- **Typical Time:** 1-48 hours (usually within 24 hours)
- **Action:** Wait. Meta is reviewing your app.

### ❌ **Rejected**
- Red X icon
- Status: "Rejected" or "Needs Changes"
- **Action:** Check rejection reason and resubmit with fixes.

### ⚠️ **Action Required**
- Orange warning icon
- Status: "Additional Information Needed"
- **Action:** Meta needs more info from you. Check notifications.
