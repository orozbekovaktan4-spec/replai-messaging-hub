## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**From unslop methodology:**
> "The difference between slop and craft is where the richness goes. Slop spreads color and effects over the chrome. Craft keeps the chrome quiet and puts the richness into content that people remember."

For REPLAI:
- **Chrome (quiet):** Navigation, buttons, headers use neutrals + one accent
- **Rich moment:** Message preview feed with platform-colored indicators
- **Signature detail:** Platform logos and connection status
