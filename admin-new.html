Instagram Connection
Connection instructions
1. Prepare the profile (in the Instagram app)
Before clicking the connect button on your computer, make sure of two things on your phone:
1. Account type:
You need a Business Account or Creator Account. A personal account will not work
How to switch:
  • Open Instagram Settings
  • Go to Account type and tools
  • Switch to a professional account
2. Message access:
You need to allow services to read your messages
Where to enable it:
  • Settings
  • Messages and story replies
  • Message requests folder
  • Allow access to messages. It should be enabled.
2. Connect Instagram
📘 Instagram Professional account required
Connect a Business or Creator account directly through Instagram Login.
No Facebook Page link is required for this flow.
⚠️ If you see "Reconnect" dialog:
  1. Click "Edit settings" if Meta shows it
  2. Make sure ALL Instagram permissions are checked
  3. Then click "Continue"
Click the "Connect Instagram" button below
Login with Instagram and authorize the permissions
Your Instagram will connect directly through Instagram Login
Grant all requested permissions
If everything goes well, you will see: "Connection established successfully"
✅ Why Instagram Login?
This connects your professional Instagram account directly through Instagram's consent screen.
  • ✅ Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • ✅ Official Meta Graph API
  • ✅ No password storage needed
  • ✅ Long-lived token (~60 days)

Open the scanner in WhatsApp
Next steps depend on your phone (Android or iPhone).
🤖
Android
  1. Open WhatsApp
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner
  3. Select Linked devices
  4. Tap Link a device
📝 If prompted, unlock your phone (fingerprint or PIN).
🍎
iPhone (iOS)
  1. Open WhatsApp
  2. Go to Settings ⚙️ (bottom right)
  3. Select Linked devices
  4. Tap Link a device
📝 Use Face ID or Touch ID if requested.
